The same one-factor-at-a-time design as the 4-Sept campaign, but on small instances (J = 7–17) with every test case listed individually. No means, no aggregation — each row is one instance, so per-instance wins, losses and outliers stay visible instead of being averaged away.

Two grids were capped by hard generator constraints, not by choice. n_prc stops at 4: with n_tsk≤5 and the per-task cap γ=0.5, U_M = α·N_prc = 4.0 at 8 cores cannot be split over 5 tasks (max 2.5). And n_tsk starts at 3, because the ≥3-distinct-periods rule cannot be met with fewer tasks. k_max=2 is likewise the floor — k_max=1 gives only periods {10,20}, two distinct values.
v3, v4 and v5a lack Phase 6b (the frequency↔segment trade), so their columns conflate a mapping change with a missing phase. Audit issue B2, still open.
