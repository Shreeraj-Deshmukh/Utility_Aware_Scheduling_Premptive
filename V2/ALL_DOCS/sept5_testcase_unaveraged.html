Why best-of-3. A single timing can be wrong by a large factor. In the 4-Sept sweep heuristic_v7 recorded 90.695 s on an instance that replays in 0.27 s with byte-identical output — the OS had descheduled the process. The minimum across repeats is the run least polluted by unrelated system activity, so it is the honest estimator of how long the work actually takes.
Why censored runs are excluded, not averaged. When a solver hits the time limit, its recorded time is the CAP, not its cost — the true value is unknown and larger. Averaging those in makes a solver that times out look faster than one that finishes. Every censored run is counted separately in the tables and contributes to no statistic.
