1. Live issues

Every entry was re-checked against the tree on 12 Sep 2026. “Evidence” means a measurement taken that day, not a claim carried from an earlier document.

Critical usrt/online/controller.py:273, 299, 314 · root cause usrt/online/dp.py:111

ONLINE-FREQ — the online phase revises the frequency of a job that has already started

Paper §II: once a job starts executing it cannot execute with a different frequency. The online DP chain is ordered by EDF ((deadline, release, task, job)), and on_completion re-plans every job at chain position > c, writing a new freq_idx at three sites.

Under non-preemptive execution, “later in the chain” implies “not yet started”, and those writes are safe. Under preemptive EDF it does not: a long-period job can start, be preempted by a shorter-period release, and still sit downstream in deadline order — so it becomes eligible for revision after it has already run.

Evidence. Verified live 12 Sep 2026 with a reproducer (ALL_DOCS/repro_online_freq_inflight.py): violation observed in 3 of 4 configurations.
Impact. Any online result where a preempted job’s frequency was revised is inadmissible under §II, and both its reported energy and its DBF check are computed at the wrong frequency. This invalidates affected online numbers rather than merely degrading them.
Medium usrt/phases/freq_trade.py

6B-INERT — Phase 6b never fires

Phase 6b is the shadow-price frequency↔segment trade (λ·ΔE > μ·ΔT), credited in its own docstring with closing a gap that leaves the heuristic 40–58% below the exact optimum once energy binds. It does not fire.

Evidence. 0 moves in 72 instances (4 Sep) and 0 in 20 instances (12 Sep re-check), across ρ = 0.4–1.0. In the 4 Sep run λ was positive — something was energy-blocked — in 26 of 72 instances, and still no move was made.

Three causes, traced on a live instance:

  • μ is a global price for a per-processor resource. With μ=0 the model concludes “time is free”. In the traced case the chosen donor freed exactly the energy the target needed and spent exactly the window slack the target needed — on the same core. Net: zero segments added.
  • Atomicity. λ·ΔE > μ·ΔT is a marginal, continuous criterion, but segments are indivisible. One downshift must free an entire shortfall or it yields nothing. Phase 5’s case ii.B handles this correctly by stacking donors until gained ≥ need; 6b makes exactly one downshift per round.
  • It gives up immediately — break on the first non-improving round rather than trying the next-best candidate.
Not a regression risk. 6b keeps a best-so-far and restores it, so it provably cannot return a worse schedule. The cost is that a documented gap is recorded as closed when it is not. The 40–58% figure predates the spec.py rewrite and has never been re-measured against the current generator.
Medium usrt/phases/swap.py:126

SWAP-BREAK — Phase 6 aborts the whole search on one failed candidate

When the best candidate fails check_all_timing, the else-branch executes break, ending the local search and discarding every remaining improving candidate from the same scan.

The deeper point. min_slack_for_job evaluates exactly the windows affected by adding time to the receiver, so the revert path should be unreachable. If it ever fires, that signals a state-consistency bug elsewhere — which makes silently breaking doubly wrong. Fix: blacklist the pair and continue, and assert or log loudly on the supposedly-unreachable revert. Fixing only the break hides a real bug.
Medium usrt/solvers/heuristic_v3.py, v4.py, v5a.py (missing) vs v5b, v6, v7, claudeoptimal (present)

V5AB-COMPARE — the variant ladder is not a controlled comparison

Phase 6b is applied to 4 of 7 heuristic variants: present in v5b, v6, v7, claudeoptimal; absent from v3, v4, v5a.

Evidence. 12 Sep 2026, direct grep of phase_freq_utility_trade across all seven solvers.
Consequence. v5a vs v5b is read as “Refine 2a vs 2b”, but it is really “2a without 6b vs 2b with 6b”. The “one capability per step” reading of the ladder no longer holds. Note: given 6B-INERT above, 6b currently contributes nothing measurable, so the confound may be nominal rather than actual — but the ladder should still be made honest.

Low / latent 18 sites · heuristic_v1..v7, ilp_v2/v3/v4, adapters.py, online/controller.py, ILP.py

FREQ-SET — processors[0]['frequencies'] assumed for every processor

Paper §VII.A(i) generates a distinct frequency set per processor and forces f_max = 1.0 on only some. The code reads processors[0] in 18 places. Latent only because generate.py currently gives every processor the same list.

Related. repair.py:44 and refine_mapping.py:75 use raw e_m as demand, i.e. they assume max(frequencies) == 1.0. A set topping out at 0.9 makes both DBF checks optimistic. Blocks paper §VII.A fidelity; costs nothing today.
Low testcase.py

C8-HARMONIC — the hand-written testcase is not harmonic

Periods are 10, 20, 20, 40, 60, 120. The paper assumes a harmonic chain, and 40 ∤ 60, so this instance sits outside the stated model. Still true on 12 Sep.

The generator is correct — usrt/gen/spec.py builds periods as base × 2^m, so every generated set is harmonic. Only the legacy hand-written testcase.py violates it. Since that file is the default for run.py and appears throughout the docs, any single-instance figure quoted from it is off-model. Cheapest fix: change 60 to 80.
Low usrt/solvers/heuristic_v6.py

DEAD-CODE — unused imports in heuristic_v6

Three names are imported and never used: compute_energy_slack, estimate_operating_freq, is_energy_bound.

Evidence. AST scan, 12 Sep 2026.
Half of the original claim (22Aug F2) is false. It also said greedy_leftshift.py and greedy_state.py were “possibly orphaned”. They are not — both are imported by heuristic_v1 and heuristic_v2, and deleting them would break run.py. Ready: delete three import names, nothing else.
Low 25 sites · usrt/**

INT-PERIOD — int(t['p_i']) silently truncates non-integer periods

Periods are coerced with int() in 25 places. A period of 10.7 becomes 10 with no warning, changing the hyper-period, the job count and every deadline. Harmless while all generators emit integers; a silent corruption if that ever changes.

Cheapest fix: validate once at load time in load_testcase and raise, rather than truncating in 25 places.

4. Examined, not defects

Each was investigated and dismissed with evidence. Recorded so the investigation is not repeated.

ClaimSourceWhy it is not a defect
Phase 5 sorts by u_i, paper §V.5 says “utility density” GREEDY-ORDER u_i is the correct key. Phase 5 decides one marginal segment: gain is u_i·w, cost is w·g(f) energy or w/f time, so utility-per-energy is u_i/g(f) and utility-per-time is u_i·f — w cancels in both. Measured over 16 instances: u_i 91.271, density 91.278, u_i/g(f) 90.957, u_i·f 91.271 — a 0.35% spread, and u_i is identical to u_i·f in all 16 runs. Utility density answers Phase 1b’s mapping question instead.
Preemption model conflict — DBF over-certifies if segments are atomic Jun C7 (“Critical”) Decided, on two independent arguments. (1) Paper §II says Preemptive. (2) Quantitatively, the blocking term implied by segment-atomic execution reaches 245% of the shortest period in dry_run_tc (largest segment 8.580 at f_min=0.35 → 24.514 against min p = 10), so the atomic reading would make nearly every generated instance infeasible. DBF is the correct test and the code is right. It is Key_Notes&Deductions.txt that is stale (“Preemption is NOT allowed mid-segment”). Action: fix the note, not the code.
Frequency is inert — DVFS looks unimplemented online 15 Aug A regime effect, not a bug. At B=400 energy is nowhere near binding, so there is no headroom up (already at f_max) and no incentive down. The lever wakes up at tight budgets, where the DP demonstrably fires the paper’s Option 3/4 conversions.
Utility does not depend on frequency15 Aug By design — paper Eq. 8 has no f term. Frequency is purely a time↔energy exchange rate. This is what makes donating energy cost a donor zero utility.
n_prc sweep co-varies n_tsk22Aug C2 Deliberate and documented in _tasks_for_cores: U_M = α·N_prc grows with the core count, so a task set must grow with the system or every task is pinned at the per-task cap γ.
Orphaned phase modules greedy_leftshift.py, greedy_state.py 22Aug F2 (half) Not orphaned at the time — both are imported by heuristic_v1 and heuristic_v2. Those two solvers were descoped by owner decision on 12 Sep 2026 (“too basic to keep considering”), so the modules are now orphaned in scope terms and would become literally so if the solvers are deleted — see the fixed ledger below. The other half of F2 — three unused imports in heuristic_v6 — is live; see DEAD-CODE above.
